New Patients

What to Expect

Starting care with a new psychiatric provider can feel overwhelming. Our goal is to make the process as simple and comfortable as possible. During your first appointment, you will have an opportunity to discuss your concerns, goals, history, and current needs with your provider. This initial visit helps us better understand how we can support you.

Before Your First Appointment

Complete the required new patient forms.

Provide your contact information.

Provide your insurance information, if applicable.

Have a valid photo ID available.

Prepare a current list of medications you are taking.

Bring relevant medical or psychiatric records, if applicable.

Write down any questions or concerns you would like to discuss.

New Patient Forms

To help us prepare for your visit, please complete the required forms before your appointment.

Insurance & Payment

Insurance coverage and payment requirements may vary. Please verify your benefits and coverage with your insurance provider before your appointment.
