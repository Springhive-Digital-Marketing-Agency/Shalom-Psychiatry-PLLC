Clinical appointments and evaluations at 24-7 Shalom Psychiatry PLLC are currently provided exclusively for adult patients (ages 18 and older). Patients must be physically present within Texas, New Mexico, Nevada, or Arizona during every virtual appointment.

What to Expect During Your Evaluation

Your initial 50-minute comprehensive evaluation is a collaborative, in-depth diagnostic clinical interview. Dr. Peace will explore:

  • Current emotional, cognitive, and physical symptoms
  • Developmental and academic history from childhood
  • Past medical history, sleep patterns, and lifestyle
  • Diagnostic clarification and shared treatment choices

Telehealth Requirements & Etiquette

To ensure clinical safety, privacy, and regulatory compliance across state lines:

  • Physical Location: Must be physically located in TX, NM, NV, or AZ at appointment time.
  • Private Space: A confidential, closed-door room free from other listeners.
  • Stable Connection: High-speed internet with functioning camera and microphone.
  • Safety Boundary: Appointments cannot be conducted while operating a motor vehicle.

Medication & Refill Policies

Safe, professional prescribing practices guided by state and federal standards:

  • Controlled Substances: Schedule II stimulants require regular scheduled video visits and PDMP checks.
  • Refill Requests: Require 48 business hours advance notice via the Kareo portal.
  • No Automatic Refills: Prescriptions are managed collaboratively during follow-ups.
  • E-Prescribing: Prescriptions sent electronically to your verified local pharmacy.
