How does telehealth work in Nevada?

You can attend your sessions from the comfort of your home using our secure telehealth platform. You must be physically located in Nevada at the time of your appointment.


Can you prescribe medication across state lines?

Our providers hold specific licenses for Nevada, allowing them to prescribe necessary psychiatric medications (subject to state and federal regulations) directly to your local pharmacy.
