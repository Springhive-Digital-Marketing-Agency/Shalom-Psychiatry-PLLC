Depression & Mood

Understanding Depression and Mood

Compassionate, comprehensive psychiatric care to help lift the weight of depression and restore your quality of life. We provide a safe, supportive environment where you can openly discuss your struggles and begin a meaningful journey toward lasting recovery.

What is Depression and Mood?

Major Depressive Disorder is a serious medical illness that negatively affects how you feel, the way you think, and how you act. It causes feelings of sadness and a loss of interest in activities you once enjoyed.

Common Symptoms

  • Persistent sad, anxious, or empty mood
  • Loss of interest in hobbies and activities
  • Changes in appetite or significant weight changes
  • Fatigue, decreased energy, or difficulty sleeping

Evaluation & Diagnosis

Our providers take the time to understand your unique experience, medical history, and specific symptoms to accurately diagnose depression and identify any co-occurring conditions.

Our Treatment Approach

We utilize a holistic approach, often combining carefully monitored antidepressant medication with lifestyle recommendations and referrals for supportive psychotherapy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are antidepressants safe to take long-term?

For many patients, long-term use is safe and necessary to prevent relapse. Your provider will continually monitor your progress and adjust treatment as needed.


What if my depression doesn't respond to medication?

Treatment resistance can happen. We specialize in exploring alternative pharmacological strategies, adjunctive therapies, and comprehensive lifestyle changes.


How soon will I start feeling better after starting antidepressants?

Some improvements in sleep and appetite may occur within the first week or two, but it generally takes 4 to 6 weeks to feel the full mood-lifting effects of the medication.
